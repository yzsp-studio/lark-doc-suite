## 🇨🇳 中文

由 14 个技能组成的文档与知识工作套件，围绕文档、表格、PDF/Word、医学文献、数据分析、个股日报与分镜提示词展开。lark-doc、word、pdf、sheet 覆盖在线文档、Office Word、PDF 以及本地或在线表格的读取、创建、编辑、转换与计算。医学线包含循证问答、临床决策支持、文献检索、单篇文献解读与中英文献翻译，回答时结合指南与文献并标注来源。data-analysis 对结构化业务数据做口径核验、定向筛选、异动归因、漏斗留存与可审计报告，daily-stock 输出单只股票的涨跌归因日报。identity 按官方口径回答会员与付费服务、隐私安全、记忆功能等产品规则问题，stage-to-story-chain 与 seedance-storyboard-grid-to-video 则把创作串成链路：双 MD 与视觉资产、契约门禁与审计链，再到九宫格故事板转视频——多个技能按任务接力，先路由再读取，各走各自合适的引擎。

## 🇬🇧 English

A 14-skill documentation and knowledge-work suite spanning documents, spreadsheets, PDF and Word handling, medical literature, business data analysis, single-stock daily reports and storyboard prompting. lark-doc, word, pdf and sheet read, create, edit, convert and compute across online docs, Office Word, PDF and both local and cloud sheets. The medical line covers evidence-based patient Q&A, clinical decision support, guideline and paper search, single-paper interpretation and English-Chinese literature translation, grounding answers in literature and citing sources. data-analysis verifies definitions, filters, judges rules and thresholds, attributes metric anomalies and audits reports, while daily-stock explains one stock's move through price action, fund flows, announcements, sector linkage, technicals and risk. identity answers product-rule questions such as membership and paid services, privacy and memory features from official positions, and stage-to-story-chain plus seedance-storyboard-grid-to-video chain creative work from dual markdown and visual assets through contract gates and audit trails to grid-storyboard-to-video — the skills are designed to hand off to each other, routing first and then reading.

> ⚠️ **A cooperating suite of 14 skills** — they reference each other; use them as a group.

### ✨ What it does

- lark-doc / word / pdf / sheet: read, write, edit, convert, compute and model across online docs, Word, PDF and local or cloud spreadsheets
- Evidence-based medical line: patient health Q&A, clinical decision support, guideline and literature search, single-paper interpretation and Chinese-English translation
- data-analysis: take Excel, CSV, PDF, image or multiple business inputs and verify definitions, judge rules and thresholds, attribute anomalies and audit findings
- daily-stock: a single-stock daily report covering the move, fund flows, announcements, sector linkage, technicals, expectations and risks
- stage-to-story-chain: a chain state machine with contract gates and an audit trail, supporting self-healing, state recovery and dual-markdown visual assets
- seedance-storyboard-grid-to-video: generate a numbered storyboard grid sheet first, then feed that sheet to Seedance as reference for the video
